3D animation: UR5e + Epson SCARA kitting cell

UR5e + Epson SCARA T6 kitting cell (Webots R2025a), one full cycle: eight trays, the outfeed cart trip, and an operator walking into the scanner fields.

Infeed conveyor → Epson T6-602S places 6 parts per tray → UR5e swaps the full tray for an empty one from the magazine → full cart (2×2×2 trays) leaves through the scanner-guarded opening.
Left click: orbit · right click: pan · wheel: zoom · bottom bar: play / scrub.
